Video commands:
  open video  Show and play the background
  close video Hide the background
  play        Play the background
  pause       Pause the background
  toggle      Toggle playback
  next        Play the next clip
  prev        Play the previous clip
  status      Show playback status
  list        List available clips
  skip        Skip seconds (default: 10)
  back        Go back seconds (default: 10)
  seek        Seek to seconds or m:ss
  goto        Load a clip by id
